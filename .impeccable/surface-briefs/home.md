# Surface brief — site

**Scope:** five pages — Home, Services, Products, About, Contact. Mode: Persuade.

**Audience and job:** B2B procurement and manufacturing engineers in Delhi NCR. They arrive with a drawing, material, thickness, quantity, and deadline, and must decide whether this shop can make the part before they email.

**Action:** send a drawing to `info@vijay-enterprises.com`. No form, no phone, no online ordering.

**Proof/content:** confirmed capacities (laser 25 mm; shear 12 mm × 3 m; TL4020; CNC Press Brake Hawk 150-31; Genius NC shear), 30+ years, confirmed products and services, Tughlakabad address. Missing photos, testimonials, logos, certifications, and phone are not invented. Internet raster may be atmosphere only, never claimed as own work.

**Constraints:** Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4, English only, contact details and map only, Delhi NCR reach, accessible and reduced-motion safe.

**Chosen direction:** The Flat Pattern. The site is a fabrication drawing that folds into the finished part. Ivory drafting paper, graphite-blue linework, red dimension ink, steel-gray notes.

**Memorable moment:** the hero flat pattern folds into a bracket on scroll; a thickness selector recalibrates the process limits.

**Unresolved:** real facility photos, named references, certifications, and a public phone number are still unavailable.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A procurement drawing that becomes the finished part; refuses the dark stock-photo factory hero and the service-card grid.

**OWN-WORLD:** Ivory drafting paper with a 24 px grid, graphite-blue rules and linework, oxide-red dimension ink, steel-gray annotations; tabbed sheet navigation, dimension callouts, title blocks, and one folded bracket as the only object.

**STORY:** The buyer sees the one-roof chain, checks real capacity against a thickness, understands what to send, and emails a drawing without wondering whether the shop can make it.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed drawing sheet: business and drawing number at the top, “Send the drawing. We return the part.” at display scale, one dimensioned flat pattern with red bend lines, five process notes, and a red title-block cell that opens an email. No split hero and no card row.
